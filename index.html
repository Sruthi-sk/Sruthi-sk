The Mind Behind
the Models

My work spans end-to-end development of machine learning systems across both research and applied domains.

I design and optimize models for time-series forecasting, image classification, self-supervised segmentation, and Bayesian modeling, and I've trained and fine-tuned transformer-based models for vision and language. Lately my focus is technical AI safety — chain-of-thought monitorability, mechanistic interpretability, and the metacognition of large language models.

Earlier work runs from low-latency pipelines for embedded neurotechnology and seizure prediction at the bedside, to quantification models for medical diagnostics, reinforcement-learning models of spatial cognition, and biologically inspired benchmarks for evaluating frontier models.
